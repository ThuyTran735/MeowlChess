Update without starting over

      
This folder installation needs a manual update. Keep the same folder and the same MeowlChess card in your browser.

      
    
        
  1. Save a backup
    Use  above for a copy of your library and preferences.
    2. 
        
  2. Download and extract
    Get MeowlChess.zip, then choose Extract All… on Windows or double-click it on macOS.
    3. 
        
  3. Replace the files in your existing folder
    Open the new extension folder and copy its contents into the extension folder you originally installed. Choose to replace existing files.
    Your existing folderMeowlChess / extension / manifest.json
    The final folder directly contains manifest.json. Avoid making an extra extension / extension folder. Your browser's MeowlChess Details page may show its installed path.
    4. 
        
  4. Reload, then refresh
    Open your browser's extensions page and click the circular Reload button on your existing MeowlChess card. Then refresh Chess.com or Lichess and reopen your review tab.
    brave://extensions
    Paste this address into the address bar of that browser.
