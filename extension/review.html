    Quick start

    1 of 3

    Choose a game

    Open Games to find completed Chess.com or Lichess games by username. You can also import a PGN or try the sample.

    Review it

    Choose Review game to analyze it on your device. Settings lets you choose the engine and review speed.

    Explore the review

    Reviews open at move 0 on Summary; choose Moves and use ← / → to step through the game, or open Engine lines for suggestions.

    Use Try again or Practice to revisit mistakes, and find saved reviews under Games.
