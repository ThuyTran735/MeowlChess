About these estimates

Game rating is a rough, uncalibrated estimate of this game's moves, not an account Elo. It needs at least eight non-book decisions with a choice of moves in competitive positions.

Opening covers up to the first 10 moves by each side from the normal starting position. Endgame begins when little non-pawn material remains; the rest is Middlegame. Custom starting positions begin in Middlegame or Endgame. Icons summarize move quality in each phase.
